## Clip 1 — "Protein Stops Filling You Up"  · 18.5s · `royalty.mp3`
- **Burned-in hook:** `PROTEIN STOPS FILLING YOU UP`
- **Single point:** Once you eat *enough* protein it stops being satiating — loses its "magical satiating effect," so your brain still craves fat/carbs. "Just eat more protein" fails.
- **Structure:** Hook (why not just eat more protein? → protein is extremely filling) → Point (the reversal) → Hook B (brain still craves fat/carbs) → Reinforce (I tested it, I failed).

> "Why not just eat more protein? Protein is extremely filling, right? But once you have enough protein, it's no longer satiating. It no longer has this magical satiating effect. If you just eat tons and tons of protein, your brain still is like, we're lacking fat or carbs. I won't stay full very long. I tested it myself. I failed on all that stuff."

## Clip 2 — "The Food Monkey"  · 16.9s · `night_drive.mp3`
- **Burned-in hook:** `THE FOOD MONKEY IS REAL`
- **Single point:** Even eating perfectly clean, your *mind* won't stop obsessing over food — a small strategic treat is what silences it.
- **Structure:** Hook (I'd literally have the food monkey) → Point (clean eating doesn't quiet the mind) → Hook B (hunger's easy, the mind is the battle) → Reinforce (the Rice Krispies removed it).

> "I'd get the food monkey. I would literally have the food monkey. So I'd be eating clean, but my mind would still have cravings and want food and think about food. Hunger in the stomach is really easy — but when your mind is just so occupied with food, [it] didn't hit the spot, right? That's why I started doing the Rice Krispies. It would remove the food monkey. I'd eat that a bit — wow, we're good."

- **Single point:** Perfect dieting is a myth — literally everyone (the coach included) blows past their calories; the skill is recovering, not being perfect.
- **Structure:** Hook (everyone goes over their calories — everyone) → Point (humans not robots, everyone messes up) → Reinforce (adherence is the real game) → Reinforce (even I screwed up for weeks, then lock in).

Everyone messes up. 50% of coaching fat loss is just focusing on adherence. It's so hard, it is crazy hard to literally be on your calories. I would spend many weeks screwing up, going over — and then you just lock in."
