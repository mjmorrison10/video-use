## Clip 1 — "Two Six-Figure Businesses, Zero Luck"  · 29.6s · `royalty.mp3`
- **Hook:** `TWO SIX-FIGURE BUSINESSES, ZERO LUCK`
- **Point:** Two six-figure businesses weren't luck — he copied a proven framework (*The Million Dollar Weekend*); rule #1 is it has to be proven to work *right now, this month*.
- Structure: Hook (failing at business? there's a reason) → credibility (two six-figure, not luck) → payoff (ripped off the framework / the book) → urgency (start in 48 hrs) → the rule.

## Clip 2 — "Can You Get One Person To Pay"  · 18.7s · `los_santos.mp3`
- **Hook:** `CAN YOU GET ONE PERSON TO PAY`
- **Point:** The only real validation is money — spend one day testing whether real people will pay; if you can't get anyone to sign up or buy, scrap it.
- Structure: Hook (people pay for hot sauce / jerky — can you get one person to pay you in a day?) → Point (if they pay, go forward) → Hook B (if you can't get anyone to sign up or buy) → Reinforce (it's not a good thing — scrap it, move on).

## Clip 3 — "Stop Overthinking It"  · 19.6s · `mask_off.mp3`
- **Hook:** `STOP OVERTHINKING IT`
- **Point:** Overthinking / waiting for perfect is why most wannabe entrepreneurs are still stuck years later — pick something, start this weekend, you can always stop.
- Structure: Hook (overthink + wait for perfect → 3 years later, still haven't started) → Point (that's almost every wannabe founder — no action) → Hook B (figure it out this weekend, start growing it) → Reinforce (you can always stop — do not overthink, just go).

- **Hook:** `BUILD ON YOUR UNFAIR ADVANTAGE`
- **Point:** Don't chase trends — start from your OWN unfair advantages and strengths (something you enjoy, that's scalable); avoid what you're bad at.
- Structure: Hook (before you pick a business, start with your unfair advantages) → Point (everyone's good at something / bad at something) → Hook B (the 4-Hour Workweek lesson: work on your strengths) → Reinforce (…because I'm terrible at spelling — can't do that).
