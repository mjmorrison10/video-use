# 5 Core Investment Principles — Shorts

Source: Financial Tortoise — a calm, sensible passive-investing creator. The long video walks through 5 principles (diversify, simplify, keep costs low, minimize taxes, play long) with a mix of talking-head desk pieces and branded graphic slides (quote cards, comparison charts, timelines). Transcript supplied by the client (Whisper large-v3, word-level).

House style: 9:16 1080×1920 30fps · Big Shoulders Bold ALL-CAPS (punctuation stripped, number commas/decimals/%/$ kept) · yellow power words · burned-in hook · HDR-style colour grade · voice-forward ~−13 LUFS · one distinct instrumental per clip. Every cut snapped to silence; captions centered.

**Selection:** the client supplied 10 moments — 3 historically proven hooks, 7 AI. Per your standing preference the proven hooks were prioritized: all 3 are used (the Thornton Wilder "manure" quote, "protect from the next big losers", and the Bezos 7-year quote), plus the strongest AI angle (the fee math). The 4 clips deliberately span 4 different principles so the batch isn't repetitive.

**Framing:** this creator's non-talking-head beats are branded graphic slides that don't fill a 9:16 frame, so — same as the last batch — every graphic beat is covered with full-frame B-roll (finance/thematic stock footage) with his narration underneath, and his face is cover-cropped whenever he's actually on camera. Center captions, crossfade transitions, no blurred backgrounds.

## Clip 1 — "Money Is Like Manure"  · ~20s · `interstellar.mp3`  *(proven #1)*
- **Hook:** `MONEY IS LIKE MANURE`
- **Principle:** Diversify.
- Structure: Hook (money is like manure — worth nothing unless spread around) → Point (spread your risk across companies, industries, and countries) → Reinforce (don't look for the needle, just buy the haystack).
- Visuals: growth B-roll → his talking head (the diversification point) → a hay field for "buy the haystack."

## Clip 2 — "Protect From The Big Losers"  · ~34s · `dark_knight.mp3`  *(proven #2 + Nokia)*
- **Hook:** `PROTECT FROM THE BIG LOSERS`
- **Principle:** Diversify (concentration risk).
- Structure: Hook (it's not just riding the winners — it's protecting from the losers) → Story (in 2000 Nokia owned 40% of the market, $250B cap, "untouchable") → Story (the iPhone arrived and Nokia lost ~90%) → Reinforce (you own the Nokias and the NVIDIAs — you can't know which is which).
- Visuals: his face (the hook) → vintage phone → smartphone → market B-roll.

## Clip 3 — "The Worst Time To Invest"  · ~28s · `royalty.mp3`  *(proven #3 Bezos)*
- **Hook:** `THE WORST TIME TO INVEST`
- **Principle:** Play long.
- Structure: Hook (October 2007, the market peak, right before the worst crisis since the Depression) → Crash (by March 2009 your $100k → $58k, nearly half gone) → Payoff (by 2024 it had grown to over half a million) → Lesson (Bezos: a 7-year time horizon means you're barely competing).
- Visuals: trading desk → market crash → market recovery → hourglass (the long game).

## Clip 4 — "1% Fee Costs You $235K"  · ~37s · `night_drive.mp3`  *(AI — fee math)*
- **Hook:** `1% FEE COSTS YOU $235K`
- **Principle:** Keep costs low.
- Structure: Hook (two investors, each $100k at 8% — the only difference is fees) → Point (one pays 0.04%, the other pays 1%) → Payoff (after 30 years: a million vs $760k — a $235k gap) → Kicker (price is what you pay, value is what you get).
- Visuals: cash → trading floor → market growth → cash.
